The application

The application will be a single page audiovisual experience.

The content will be narrated stories backed with random video clips and a music score.

Each story will be in its own chapter. Each chapter will have a short title sequence, the story section and a short credits section. The next story will automatically follow.

Story selection will be randomised, as will be the story order. The video clips behind each story will be randomly selected, as will their edit points. The background music will be randomly selected from a reasonably large library of tracks.

The assets

  1. UIDs of publicly available videos from Youtube along with metadata with each video title and Youtube channel name.
  2. Narrated audio file recordings of my stories in mp3 or similar format along with metadata including: Story title; date of publication; length in seconds; a full text transcript.
  3. Music files in mp3 format, which I have written and recorded.

Pages

The application will be a single HTML page only. It will have three views with their associated areas and non-visible/audio components.

Views

  1. Title
  2. Main video
  3. Credits

The application will have the following view areas and components:

  1. Titles title text area.
  2. Titles video content area.
  3. Titles audio component.
  4. Main video Volume control, including slider and mute.
  5. Main video transport controls, including pause, play and next.
  6. Main video presentation area.
  7. Main video music audio component.
  8. Main video narration audio component.
  9. Main video narration subtitles area.
  10. Credits text area.
  11. Credits video area.
  12. Credits music component.
